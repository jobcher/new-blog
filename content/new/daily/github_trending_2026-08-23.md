#### OpenAI 回应 Codex 使用额度差异
<p><b>OpenAI 回应 Codex 使用额度差异</b><br><br>我们调查了一些关于 <b>Codex 使用额度存在差异</b> 的反馈。对于这类事情，我们不会在没有与社区沟通、没有保持透明的情况下擅自进行调整。<br><br>不过，我们发现，在与受到影响的用户沟通后，其中很多人都在使用 <b>sub2api</b>。把订阅账户的额度转换成 API 流量，然后再将这些流量重新提供给其他人，或者在多个用户之间共享，并不是我们所支持的使用方式。这类行为也会被我们的<b>反欺诈系统</b>标记出来。<br><br>如果你是通过 <b>“使用 ChatGPT 登录（Sign in With ChatGPT）”</b> 来使用自己的订阅，那么完全没有问题。无论是通过官方客户端，还是通过许多支持使用你的 ChatGPT 账户登录并使用订阅所包含额度的开源客户端（例如 <b>Pi、OpenCode 等</b>），都属于正常支持的使用方式。<br><br><b>原文:</b><br></p><blockquote expandable="">We've investigated a few messages about codex usage limits being different. That's not something we change without engaging the community and being transparent.<br><br>What we did see is that when talking to affected users many were using sub2api. Converting a subscription into api traffic to then re-serve or share across many users is not something we support and this type of usage gets flagged by our fraud-prevention systems.<br><br>You are completely fine if you use your subscription through Sign in With ChatGPT, either through the official clients or through one of the many OSS clients (Pi, OpenCode, ...) that support signing in with your account and using your included usage.</blockquote><br><br>消息来源: <a href="https://x.com/thsottiaux/status/2090675027670978569?s=20" target="_blank" rel="noopener" onclick="return confirm('Open this link?\n\n'+this.href);">X</a><p></p>
